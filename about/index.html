About JYGEN

Technology For Real Use.

JYGEN is a future focused technology company. We design and build software, digital products, AI solutions, automation, IoT, SaaS and custom systems around meaningful problems.

Technology should make work clearer, connect what matters, and remain useful after launch. That belief guides how we think, design and build.

We believe a useful product earns its place in the way people work: clear from the first interaction, dependable under pressure and ready to improve.

Our Story

Ideas Become Useful When They Are Built With Purpose.

Yogendra Haneesh P and Prasanth Varma CH started JYGEN to turn ambitious ideas into dependable technology for real use. The work starts with understanding a challenge and the people affected by it. Then we bring the right mix of software, product design and intelligent systems together to solve it.

We care about clarity at every step: what the system needs to do, how it will fit into existing work, and how it can keep improving once people begin using it.

Our Founders

The People Behind JYGEN.

Founder

Yogendra Haneesh P

Yogendra Haneesh is the Founder of JYGEN, leading Business, Product, Frontend, and Execution. He drives product direction, UI/UX, frontend development, business development, client acquisition, and client relationships, turning real world requirements into practical digital products.

Alongside JYGEN, he serves as Managing Director of quickkads.com, working across advertising, digital marketing, outdoor media, and client projects. His experience in building and executing real world projects brings a practical business perspective to the products and solutions JYGEN creates.

Co-Founder

Prasanth Varma Ch

Prasanth Varma is the Co-Founder of JYGEN, leading Engineering, Backend, and Technical Development. He oversees frontend and complete backend development, application architecture, databases, APIs, and core technical implementation, turning product ideas into reliable digital systems.
